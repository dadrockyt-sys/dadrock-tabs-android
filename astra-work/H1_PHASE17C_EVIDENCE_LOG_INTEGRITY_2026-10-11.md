## Specific gap addressed

The previously committed paper-only feasibility review `astra-work/h1_feasibility_review_offline_v1.py` checks declared 64-character log digests, source identifiers, stage fields and arithmetic but **does not itself open and hash the underlying evidence logs**. The new standalone review-only checker can match externally supplied bounded *local log bytes* to each receipt's declared SHA-256. This reduces the risk of accidentally accepting a mistyped, missing or modified log. A hash match still does **NOT** prove that a log is genuine, that claimed numbers are correct or conservative, that the machine is comparable, or that any reviewer has approved the run.

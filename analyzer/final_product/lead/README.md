# Lead Final-Product Engine

Lead is a separate professional engine modeled structurally on Rhythm but not musically copied from it.

Lead and Rhythm may begin from the same deterministic separated Guitar views, but Lead must diverge after separation with its own:

- Hz/frequency and pitch-trajectory features;
- melodic/solo candidate selection;
- note continuity and phrasing logic;
- six-string fretboard movement model;
- bends, bend-releases, vibrato, slides, hammer-ons, pull-offs, sustain and related Lead techniques;
- Lead-specific training datasets/models/checkpoints;
- Lead-specific quality thresholds and professional identity;
- professional six-string Lead TAB rendering.

There is no assumed or fake standalone "Lead stem". The professional Lead task is to identify Lead musical behavior inside the separated Guitar views using audio-derived evidence.

This folder remains inactive until a reference-free Lead analyzer is independently proven. Existing Lead customer behavior stays on the legacy path until then.

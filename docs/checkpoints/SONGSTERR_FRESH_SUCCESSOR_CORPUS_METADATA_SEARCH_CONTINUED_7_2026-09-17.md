## New lead — DoMP (Dataset of Monophonic Patterns)

Authoritative/public metadata located:

- Zenodo record/version DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.10818617`.
- Zenodo reports Dataset of Monophonic Patterns, version `1.0.0`, published 2024-03-14, one `DoMP.zip` file, 2.7 MB, MD5 `c5f0e2b10eae47435f409930a4fa94ae`.
- The 2024 Audio Mostly paper by Nishal Silva and Luca Turchet states that DoMP contains 4000 patterns/variations from 40 musicians, including 20 electric-guitar players, and that patterns/repetitions are represented in MIDI and audio format.
- The same paper states the electric guitars were outfitted with a Fishman TriplePlay MIDI tracker and discusses tracker limitations/errors (including techniques that can produce false notes).

Prospective correctness status:

`REJECT_REFERENCE_NOT_INDEPENDENT_FOR_NOTE_CORRECTNESS`

Reason: for the guitar subset, symbolic note events are produced by a MIDI-tracking/pitch-detection device converting the performed guitar signal into MIDI. That is not an independent direct-performance note-birth reference under the frozen rule excluding Basic Pitch **and other AMT/pitch-estimation output**. Even if the audio and MIDI are synchronized and the corpus lineage were clean, DoMP MIDI cannot serve as authoritative correctness truth for this evaluation.

No DoMP media or annotation payload was opened/downloaded in this pass.

## New lead — DoPP (Dataset of Polyphonic Patterns)

Public/primary metadata located:

- DOI/data record identifier: `10.5281/zenodo.14497998`.
- Nishal Silva's University of Trento doctoral thesis lists DoPP as 2000 symbolic pattern/repetition recordings from 20 musicians (10 guitar, 10 keyboard).
- The 2026 Journal of the Audio Engineering Society paper describes DoPP as musical patterns and expressive repetitions in **audio**, recorded by 20 musicians, created for real-time polyphonic audio-pattern detection.
- Public author material describes the guitar audio as direct, unprocessed 44.1 kHz/24-bit recordings and frames DoPP as a polyphonic **audio-pattern** corpus.

Prospective correctness status:

`REJECT_NO_VERIFIED_EXACT_NOTE_BIRTH_PLUS_MIDI_PITCH_REFERENCE`

Reason: metadata reviewed in this pass establishes polyphonic real guitar audio and pattern-level/repetition structure, but does not establish deterministic performed-note onset + integer-MIDI-pitch truth for every note tied to the actual recording. Pattern identity/equivalence labels are insufficient for this frozen correctness boundary.

No DoPP media or annotation payload was opened/downloaded in this pass.

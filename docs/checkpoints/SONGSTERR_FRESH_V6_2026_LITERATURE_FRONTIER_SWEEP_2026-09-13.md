Primary paper:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11904

`TART: A Modular Tool for Technique-Aware Audio-to-Tablature Guitar Transcription` (submitted 2026-09-10) evaluates on:
- GuitarSet;
- EGDB;
- Noisy GuitarSet; and
- Noisy EGDB.

The noisy sets are augmented derivatives of already-known sources, not new independent real performances with a fresh reference stream.

**Disposition:** no new V6 replacement corpus is introduced by this paper. GuitarSet is already revealed/closed and EGDB is already rejected under the current rights/reference-provenance evidence. Do not use noisy derivatives to restore untouched status.

## Current literature pattern

The newest guitar-transcription work reviewed continues to benchmark primarily on the same established corpus family or derivatives:
- GuitarSet;
- EGDB/EGDB-PG;
- Guitar-TECHS;
- GAPS;
- archived GOAT/reference lines;
- synthetic/augmented variants.

This sweep found **no newly published 2026 real-guitar corpus** that simultaneously establishes:
1. explicit usable performance-audio rights;
2. real guitar;
3. immutable independent performed note-level onset+pitch truth;
4. plausible >=1,000 V6-positive evidence volume without duplicate/effect inflation; and
5. defensible untouched status for this branch.

This is an evidence-bounded search result, not a proof that no such corpus exists.

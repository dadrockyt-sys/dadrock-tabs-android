### Reference mechanism

RWC’s symbolic data is not generated by Basic Pitch or another guitar AMT system. The 2026 RWC paper states:

- the original MIDI files were produced after music production by professional music transcribers from the karaoke industry;
- AIST subsequently manually aligned MIDI to audio using custom tools;
- the 2026 re-release systematically re-aligned the MIDI using SyncToolbox / DTW with chroma- and onset-based features;
- first-note and last-note anchors were manually annotated;
- all tracks were verified by listening; additional manual anchors were inserted for problematic material.

The current aligned-MIDI README records a 10 ms temporal resolution for the preprocessed aligned representation and says all alignments were manually controlled.

This is meaningfully different from datasets where note pitch truth itself originates in Aubio/YIN, Fishman TriplePlay, NeuralNote, Basic Pitch, pYIN or another AMT/pitch-tracking output.

### Remaining correctness-boundary concern

The 2026 timing alignment is not described as a fully hand-clicked onset timestamp for every note. It is a score/MIDI-to-audio synchronization result produced by DTW/onset/chroma alignment with manual verification and anchor correction.

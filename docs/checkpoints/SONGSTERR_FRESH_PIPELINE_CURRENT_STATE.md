## REPRESENTATION-SEAM ROOT CAUSE

Frozen V6 uses 2048-sample Hann frames zero-padded to FFT8192. A Hann sinusoid therefore appears as a broad deterministic main lobe on the oversampled grid, while frozen V3 synthetic fixtures use narrow line peaks and sample nearby local background within radius 6. Raw V6 Hann-lobe innovation supplied directly to V3 can make a harmonic's own leakage look like its local background.

This explains the original all-anchor support failure despite high total innovation energy. Bridge research therefore targets representation geometry only and may not relax frozen V3/V6 decision thresholds.

## CURRENT TECHNICAL CONCLUSION

The seam is no longer one undifferentiated problem. Three semantic roles are now empirically separated in the frozen synthetic population:

1. **support / local-background eligibility** — benefits from leakage-cleaned representation;
2. **observed NNLS fit evidence** — should retain richer raw V6 innovation rather than reuse sparse support evidence;
3. **candidate/template competition population** — must be broad enough to preserve competing explanations, as shown by the dyad MIDI60 versus MIDI64 comparison.

A fourth issue is orthogonal: **reattack temporal/support representation** can fail before any fit or competition rule is reached.

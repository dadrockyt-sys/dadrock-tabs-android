## Composition question

Let the four positive-core conditions for a selected proposal be:

- `S`: base selected support is available and eligible;
- `E`: frozen candidate-evidence significance passes in its original support semantics;
- `O`: no frozen credible lower-owner veto applies;
- `K`: broad raw KKT strict necessity is certified.

The question is not whether these measurements prove ground-truth note birth. They do not. The question is whether an **unvalidated precision-first research admission state** can combine them without introducing a new calibration trade-off.

## Ex-ante axioms

The following fail-closed axioms are justified from the existing research architecture rather than fixture outcomes.

### Axiom 1 — no rescue across independent protections

No evidence role may numerically compensate for failure of another independent protection.

Examples of forbidden rescue semantics include:

- large raw contribution overriding a lower-owner veto;
- high candidate-evidence significance overriding absence of raw strict contribution;
- KKT raw necessity overriding unavailable/ineligible harmonic support;
- any weighted sum in which enough strength on one channel cancels a failure on another.

Such rescue requires a cross-channel utility scale or calibration source. None exists in the frozen lineage.

### Axiom 2 — no new trade-off constant

The composition may not introduce a weight, score, probability, raw magnitude cutoff, rank cutoff, top-K, or other tunable exchange rate between the four roles.

### Axiom 3 — positive-core monotonicity

Adding a newly failed protection cannot turn a non-positive proposal positive. Conversely, an all-clear proposal is not made less admissible by the mere existence of other failing proposals.

### Axiom 4 — support failure is not proof of falsehood

Because the frozen reattack line establishes that support representation can be unresolved while broad raw contribution remains present, absence/ineligibility of selected support must not be reinterpreted as proof that the underlying note did not begin.

Therefore support-unavailable/ineligible cases are fail-closed **unresolved / not admitted**, not rescued and not promoted by raw evidence.

### Axiom 5 — research admission is not truth

Even when every positive-core condition passes, the result is only a **candidate corroboration state for future untouched external validation**. It is not a claim of calibrated correctness or customer eligibility.

## Boolean consequence

Under Axioms 1–3, if all four roles are declared required for the positive research-admission state, the only Boolean positive condition that allows no failed role to be rescued is:

`POSITIVE_CORE = S AND E AND O AND K`.

This is not selected because it fits the frozen fixture outcomes. It follows directly from the no-rescue requirement. Any OR rule, majority vote, weighted score, rank rule or compensating formula violates at least one frozen axiom or introduces a new calibration quantity.

The conjunction contains **no new numeric constant**.

## Required tri-state semantics

A defensible future mechanical composition should not collapse every non-positive case into the same scientific meaning.

The prospective semantic classes justified by this review are:

1. `POSITIVE_CORE_CANDIDATE`
   - onset/raw evidence available;
   - `S=true`, `E=true`, `O=true`, `K=true`;
   - means only that every independently frozen protection/measurement required by the positive core is clear.

2. `PROTECTION_REJECTED`
   - support is available/eligible enough for the relevant protection to be evaluated;
   - one or more of `E`, `O`, or `K` fails;
   - means the proposal is not corroborated by the positive-core contract; it does not assert ontological absence of the note.

3. `UNRESOLVED_SUPPORT_OR_CONTEXT`
   - onset evidence is unavailable, required context is unavailable, or selected base support is unavailable/ineligible;
   - no raw/KKT rescue is allowed;
   - especially preserves the unresolved status of the reattack seam rather than treating raw contribution as a fallback.

A future PRE may refine deterministic status names, but it may not change these three semantic roles without a new theory checkpoint.

## Why this does not bypass reattack

The rule does not make `K=true` sufficient when `S=false`. Therefore a reattack-like proposal with raw strict contribution but unresolved support remains `UNRESOLVED_SUPPORT_OR_CONTEXT`.

This is intentionally conservative and consistent with the current evidence boundary. It does not claim that reattacks are false. It says the present successor cannot safely admit them until the temporal/support representation is resolved or independently validated under a separately authorized method.

## Why this is materially different from post-hoc thresholding

The proposed positive-core semantics do not use:

- observed raw necessity values;
- observed KKT correlation magnitudes;
- observed fixture ranks;
- class counts from the 23 fixtures;
- a newly fitted threshold;
- a weighted score;
- a per-fixture or per-MIDI exception.

The only numeric protection retained is the already-frozen V3 evidence-significance `0.10` in its original semantics. KKT contributes only its prospectively frozen strict-necessity certificate. The composition itself is purely logical.

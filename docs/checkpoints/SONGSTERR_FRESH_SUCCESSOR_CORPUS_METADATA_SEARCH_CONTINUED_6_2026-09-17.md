### Reference-construction evidence

The 2024 MSc thesis *Automatic Guitar Transcription Using Deep Neural Networks* provides a more precise account of the dataset construction than the earlier metadata search:

- the recorded dataset was created specifically to test generalization on different genres;
- the author deliberately recorded performances from existing transcriptions, attempting to stick as closely as possible to the supplied scores rather than transcribing every performance from scratch afterward;
- MIDI/tab scores were obtained from Ultimate Guitar;
- some scores were adapted and minor errors corrected in Guitar Pro;
- recordings were then made on acoustic or clean electric guitar, using either a phone or a sound card/DAW;
- the Guitar Pro MIDI was imported into GarageBand and aligned to the recording;
- the thesis explicitly defines this alignment as synchronizing and matching the **timing and content** of the transcription to the recorded signal and says discrepancies/misalignments could be corrected;
- the aligned Guitar Pro transcription was then duplicated/split into six per-string MIDI files;
- note start time, duration and integer pitch are extracted from those six MIDI files to generate the ground-truth note/frame tablature labels.

This is materially different from GIHME/MMIP: the GM labels are not described as being generated by Basic Pitch, NeuralNote, YIN/Aubio or another AMT/pitch-estimation system. The source transcription is score-derived, but the thesis explicitly documents post-recording alignment against the audio, including timing/content correction.

Accordingly, **do not reject GM solely as AMT-derived or as completely unaligned score-only truth**. Its reference-independence status remains potentially acceptable from metadata, subject to a stable release documenting the actual delivered annotations and alignment provenance.

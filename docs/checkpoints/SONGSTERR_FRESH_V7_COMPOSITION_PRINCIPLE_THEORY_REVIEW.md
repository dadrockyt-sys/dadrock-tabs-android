## 3. PROVENANCE FINDINGS

### 3.1 Support/local-background template eligibility — ex-ante structural principle

V3 iteration 1 prospectively replaced the historical V6 fundamental/max-harmonic eligibility ratio with a multi-harmonic support rule over a fixed detuning grid. Its support/local-background mechanics, minimum supported-harmonic count and weighted coverage were frozen before the iteration result.

This is a physical/structural eligibility principle. Later diagnostics may measure it independently without treating the old support-space NNLS necessity result as part of the same semantic object.

### 3.2 Lower-owner protection — ex-ante structural principle

V3 iteration 2 prospectively introduced the lower-owner explanation guard after the iteration-1 alias failure. The guard is defined by harmonic-bin geometry and supported-exclusive counts:

- a lower candidate becomes a credible owner when it has at least two owner-exclusive supported harmonics;
- the selected candidate survives that owner only when it has at least two selected-exclusive supported harmonics;
- a credible owner with insufficient selected-exclusive support produces `LOWER_OWNER_EXPLAINS_SELECTED`.

The PRE explicitly excludes amplitude-ratio, confidence-score, per-MIDI and real-data-derived threshold rescue logic.

This remains a defensible frozen independent protection dimension.

### 3.3 Candidate-evidence significance `0.10` — ex-ante geometric rationale exists

V3 iteration 3 prospectively defined

`candidateEvidenceFraction = supportedExcessNorm / innovationNorm`

and froze `MIN_CANDIDATE_EVIDENCE_FRACTION = 0.10` before the iteration-3 result.

Unlike the historical NNLS necessity threshold, the iteration-3 PRE records an explicit geometry-based rationale independent of real correctness observations: six candidate harmonic slots occupy only a small fraction of the 4097-bin innovation vector, giving a diffuse unstructured-noise reference scale of approximately `sqrt(6 / 4097) ~= 0.038` before peak-selection inflation; `0.10` was prospectively chosen to require materially more concentrated candidate-aligned supported excess while remaining dimensionless and amplitude-scale invariant.

Therefore the frozen `0.10` evidence-significance protection has documented ex-ante provenance. This review does not change, retune, broaden or newly adopt that threshold; it only records why the already-frozen independent protection has a different provenance from raw-fit necessity.

### 3.4 Historical NNLS necessity `0.01` — prospective but representation-bound

The V5 preregistration froze `(R_without - R_full) / E >= 0.01` before V5 implementation and before a real V5 correctness result. V6 then froze the same `0.01` selected-template necessity fraction within its own onset-innovation candidate dictionary. V3 iterations inherited `0.01` explicitly as the unchanged historical necessity concept.

However, the reviewed frozen documents do not provide a representation-independent mathematical derivation for `0.01`. They establish that it was prospectively frozen and not tuned from real correctness data; they do not establish that `0.01` is invariant to candidate population, candidate-template construction, feature-bin universe, or observation representation.

That distinction is material because the later V7 diagnostics established that candidate population and feature-bin universe independently change the measured necessity, and the all-49 fixed-feature raw fit is not the same NNLS problem on which historical `0.01` was frozen.

**Conclusion:** historical `0.01` MUST NOT be transferred to the broad all-49 fixed-feature raw necessity measurement.

### 3.5 Relative rank / top-K / maximum-only — no frozen ex-ante composition principle

No reviewed prospective charter/PRE defines raw-necessity rank, top-K, maximum-only selection, or a relative-order cutoff as the successor acceptance semantics.

The later support-conditioned raw-necessity landscape was deliberately measurement-only and found selected candidates at multiple ranks across prospectively named multi-note controls. A rank/top-K rule chosen now would therefore be post-result selection, not recovery of an earlier frozen principle.

No such rule is authorized.

### 3.6 Broad all-49 fixed raw necessity — measurement exists, final acceptance semantics do not

The later fixed-feature and landscape diagnostics provide a mechanically clean broad raw contribution measurement under an all-49 candidate dictionary and a common fixed feature universe. That solves a measurement-separation problem; it does not supply a principled positive-class boundary.

No reviewed frozen theory supplies a numeric broad-raw cutoff, rank cutoff, candidate subset, composite score, or other representation-independent decision rule for this measurement.

### 3.7 Reattack temporal/support representation — independently unresolved

The original temporal/support diagnostic attempt remains mechanically successful but its stored measurement payload is inaccessible. Its first-run policy makes that attempt authoritative and prohibits a rescue rerun/reconstruction.

No reviewed theory supplies a justified fallback that would allow broad raw-fit evidence to bypass missing support/temporal representation for `reattack_m64`.

The temporal/support dimension therefore remains independently unresolved.

## 4. COMPOSITION REVIEW RESULT

The frozen lineage does contain defensible independent protection semantics:

- support/local-background physical eligibility;
- lower-owner explanation protection;
- candidate-evidence significance with the already-frozen `0.10` ex-ante geometric rationale.

It does **not** contain a defensible ex-ante rule for converting the new broad all-49 fixed-feature raw necessity measurement into a final acceptance decision.

In particular, this review does not authorize:

- historical `0.01` on the new broad raw fit;
- a new numeric raw-necessity threshold selected from the frozen 23-fixture outcomes;
- rank/top-K/maximum-only acceptance;
- a per-MIDI or per-fixture exception;
- a candidate-subset search;
- a learned or hand-weighted composite score fit to the observed synthetic outcomes;
- a reattack fallback inferred from later diagnostics.

A final synthetic successor classifier cannot be honestly frozen from the current evidence without introducing a new source of principle or calibration.

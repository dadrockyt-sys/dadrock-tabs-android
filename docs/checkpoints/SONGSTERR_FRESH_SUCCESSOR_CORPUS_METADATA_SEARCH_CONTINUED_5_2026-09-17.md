### Authoritative publication evidence

The official UMONS/ORBi copy of the 2022 SMC paper explicitly points to `https://github.com/numediart/GIHME` as the dataset repository.

The paper documents the recording path as real hexaphonic guitar, including dry/clean per-string signals, and states that played-note annotations were produced as follows:

- six automatic pitch extractions were run in parallel on the clean hexaphonic signals;
- tuning and string identity were used to infer fret number;
- Aubio's YIN-FFT implementation generated the pitch-extraction data; and
- the generated data were then manually verified to consolidate note/fret information.

The paper also says the objective annotation process was not finished at submission time.

This is material to the frozen reference-independence gate. The played-note truth is not a direct performance-control/sensor/MIDI capture independent of transcription algorithms; it originates in automatic pitch estimation and is then manually verified. Under the saved rule rejecting labels generated by Basic Pitch, NeuralNote **or other AMT systems**, GIHME cannot serve as the authoritative external correctness truth for this proposal evaluation merely because humans subsequently verified the extracted labels.

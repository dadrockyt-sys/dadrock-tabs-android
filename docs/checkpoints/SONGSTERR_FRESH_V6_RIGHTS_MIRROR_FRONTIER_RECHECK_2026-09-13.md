## Geoff Bremner Multimodal Music Corpus sample — POTENTIAL PRIVATE-LICENSE LEAD, NOT AUDIT-READY

The public Hugging Face sample states that the corpus is 100% original, owned by one creator, and commercially licensable through separate Professional/Enterprise licensing. The public sample itself is `CC BY-NC 4.0` and contains one track represented by 38 repository rows/files, including audio master, stems, MIDI, Guitar Pro, PDF notation and metadata.

This is cleaner chain-of-title evidence than most web-derived music corpora, but the public material does **not** establish:

1. that the MIDI corresponding specifically to real guitar stems is a contemporaneous/independent performed-note capture rather than authored/quantized score data or a post-production transcription;
2. exact performed guitar onset timing semantics;
3. the size of the full licensable guitar-performance population or a plausible >=1,000 V6-positive event floor;
4. immutable full-corpus identities/checksums/release contract; or
5. the exact commercial license grant that would cover this product-validation experiment.

**Disposition: keep as a metadata-only private-license lead, not an audit-ready holdout.** Do not acquire the sample/full media for V6, infer note truth from Guitar Pro/MIDI, or count unspecified private-corpus volume. It may be reconsidered only if authoritative documentation establishes guitar-specific performed MIDI/onset provenance, sufficient evidence volume, stable identities and explicit usable rights.

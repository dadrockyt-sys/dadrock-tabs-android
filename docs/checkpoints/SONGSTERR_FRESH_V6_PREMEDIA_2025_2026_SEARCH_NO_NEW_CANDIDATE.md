## EGDB-PG — not admitted as a new independent candidate

Public records inspected:

- Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.19542613`, version v1, published 2026-04-12;
- Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.19789500`, later record containing EGDB-PG/EGDB-NDSP FLAC bundles;
- public paper metadata for *Towards Generalizability to Tone and Content Variations in the Transcription of Amplifier Rendered Electric Guitar Audio* (Chen et al., 2025).

No dataset file was previewed, downloaded, or opened.

Public metadata establishes that EGDB-PG is an amplifier-rendered electric-guitar transcription dataset. The associated paper describes it as generated by re-rendering the **EGDB** dataset with a large collection of amplifier/cabinet presets. It is therefore a derivative/rendered expansion of the already-frozen EGDB line rather than a new independently collected real-performance holdout.

The current Zenodo records also do not establish a usable public dataset license: each page contains a `Rights` section with `License` but no license value. Public downloadability is not a license grant.

Access is also incomplete for the original full WAV form:

- v1 hosts a 24-preset subset and tells researchers to contact the authors for the complete dataset;
- the later record hosts FLAC bundles and tells researchers to contact the authors for the complete WAV version.

### Frozen disposition

**NOT_ADMITTED_NEW_CANDIDATE / DO_NOT_REOPEN_EGDB**

This is not a new rejection of EGDB and does not revise the prior EGDB `REJECT_PREMEDIA` decision. It records only that EGDB-PG does not qualify as a new independent untouched holdout under the current search because:

- it is derived from the already-closed EGDB line;
- its current public Zenodo records do not state a license value; and
- the full original WAV dataset is request-only rather than a prospectively frozen public payload.

No attempt was made to infer or transfer rights from EGDB to EGDB-PG, and no data was opened to investigate whether the derivative rendering might otherwise be useful.

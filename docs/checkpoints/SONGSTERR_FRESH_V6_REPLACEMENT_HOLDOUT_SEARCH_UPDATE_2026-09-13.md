## AG-PT-set — strongest current scientific lead, dataset-license blocker

Public identity:
- Zenodo record `10159492`, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.10159492`, version `v1`.
- archive `aGPTset_z.zip`, approximately 6.7 GB, MD5 `1dff8103f9ad6e1a86cee2e5e39cbe87`.
- real monophonic acoustic steel-string guitar recordings captured from internal transducers.
- 15 h 55 m total recordings across 12 expressive/percussive techniques.
- 10 h 04 m / 32,592 events across eight techniques have manually identified millisecond-level onsets.
- recordings span multiple players and acoustic guitars.

The 2024 AG-PT-set paper explicitly states that the audio files and relative metadata include **onset and pitch annotations** and are available through the Zenodo dataset. For the labeled material, the pitched techniques are Natural Harmonics, Palm Mute, Pick Near Bridge, and Pick Over the Soundhole; four additional labeled classes are percussive and therefore would not be valid pitched-reference events for the frozen V6 matcher. Public paper statistics report 2,092 Natural Harmonic events, 7,588 Palm Mute events, 7,227 Pick Near Bridge events, and 7,657 Pick Over the Soundhole events, giving ample plausible pitched event volume without counting percussive events or unlabeled techniques.

The onset labels were manually annotated by musicians at millisecond precision rather than generated by a model/detector, which is scientifically attractive for the frozen <=50 ms matcher. The corpus is monophonic/isolated-note oriented rather than long-form polyphonic music, but the existing V6 framework does not require polyphony and the event volume is well above the frozen >=1,000-positive evidence-volume threshold in principle.

**Rights blocker:** the Zenodo dataset record is public/open but its current Rights/License field is blank. The conference paper itself is CC BY 4.0, but that paper license must not be inferred to license the separate 6.7-GB audio dataset. No permissive dataset-audio license has yet been established from an authoritative source.

Disposition: **strongest current scientific lead, but NOT audit-ready and do not download the archive yet.** First obtain authoritative dataset-audio licensing terms and perform branch-specific contamination/history review. Only if rights are permissive and the corpus is untouched should we inspect metadata/reference structure further and freeze a corpus-specific reference-blind inventory/alignment preregistration before real media access.

## EG-IPT — promising real-DI lead, not audit-ready

Public identity:
- Zenodo record `15205644`, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.15205644`.
- 52,320 monophonic electric-guitar audio files, 28 h 22 m 56 s, 96 kHz / 24-bit.
- 19 playing-technique classes.
- professional real-guitar studio recording.
- six simultaneous capture paths including a BSS AR-133 DI channel.
- three Gibson SG pickup configurations.
- archive reported by Zenodo as `EG-IPT.zip`, 23.8 GB, MD5 `48a5135adfd090515ff0af7dc5c3c32f`.

Accompanying public code repository `nbrochec/nime2025` confirms that the dataset is organized under pickup configuration and that the project-generated CSV contains only `file_path`, technique `label`, and train/test/validation split. Its processing code trims silence for technique classification. The surfaced public metadata/code does **not** establish a separate immutable note-level pitch/onset annotation stream suitable for the already-frozen V6 correctness matcher.

The repository code is GPL-3.0, but that is not sufficient evidence by itself for the audio archive's dataset license. Zenodo search results available in this research pass did not expose a clear audio-license field. Therefore rights must also be resolved independently before any audit selection.

Disposition: **keep as metadata-only lead, not audit-ready**. Do not download the 23.8-GB archive or run a contamination audit yet. First establish both (a) audio-dataset license/provenance and (b) a deterministic performed pitch/onset reference contract that exists independently of model output. If either cannot be established, reject without corpus access.

## GUITAR-FX-DIST / IDMT-SMT-Audio-Effects — rejected on rights gate

GUITAR-FX-DIST is derived from the IDMT-SMT-Audio-Effects source recordings. Fraunhofer's official dataset page states that IDMT-SMT-Audio-Effects is provided for evaluation under **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**.

This is materially narrower than the permissive product-validation rights required for the V6 admission path. The corpus also consists predominantly of isolated two-second note/effect samples rather than independent musical performances.

Disposition: **REJECTED for V6 admission under the current product-validation path.** Do not download/audit/score GUITAR-FX-DIST or IDMT-SMT-Audio-Effects as a replacement holdout.

That recording provenance is not a defensible permissive-audio basis for this product-validation holdout. A separately downloadable annotation file does not grant rights to the underlying third-party recordings.

## G&N / TENT — rejected on source-recording rights

The TENT paper describes a scientifically useful manually annotated set of 42 monophonic electric-guitar tracks containing 1,113 note events. However the recordings were collected from the CD accompanying a commercial guitar textbook by Gill and Nolan (1997). The paper/code publication license does not grant rights to that underlying textbook audio.

Disposition: **REJECTED for V6 admission on source-recording provenance/rights.** Do not acquire or score the commercial-CD audio.

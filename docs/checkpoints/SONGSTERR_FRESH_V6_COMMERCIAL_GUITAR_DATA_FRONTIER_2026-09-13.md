## GRAUX / Water guitar packs — real live guitar + companion MIDI, but MIDI is not performed-note truth

Primary commercial pages reviewed:
- https://grauxmusic.com/
- https://grauxmusic.com/pages/faq
- https://grauxmusic.com/pages/royalty-fair
- https://grauxmusic.com/pages/non-exclusive-royalty-fair-license
- representative pack/product pages including `french-hits`, `dark-trap`, `rock-machine` and the pack index.

### Attractive surface facts

GRAUX states that:
- every guitar is recorded live by one named guitarist, Sébastien Graux;
- packs include WAV, dry/wet stems and MIDI;
- large bundles contain many original guitar performances;
- the material is commercially licensed for use in music releases under its Royalty-Fair terms;
- GRAUX retains ownership/originality and supplies an explicit licensing framework.

That gives a cleaner rightsholder/provenance story than anonymous sample mirrors.

### Decisive reference-semantics failure

The `FRENCH HITS` product page explicitly describes the MIDI as:

> “MIDI bass notes and chords.”

That is companion harmonic/arrangement information, not an immutable contemporaneous per-note event stream captured from the real guitar performance.

The public pages do not establish:
- every picked/plucked guitar note represented in MIDI;
- performed attack timestamps;
- performed string/fret identity;
- simultaneous hardware/sensor capture;
- unquantized raw MIDI from the guitar performance;
- one-to-one raw guitar stem/reference event mapping.

Therefore the presence of MIDI beside real live guitar WAV/stems does not satisfy the frozen V6 reference gate. Do not infer performed note truth from chord/bass MIDI, tempo, arrangement, audio or notation.

### Rights mismatch

The current Royalty-Fair license is a music-production license. It grants non-exclusive/non-transferable use of GRAUX sounds in the licensee's own musical compositions/releases, with mandatory credit and other release conditions. It also prohibits resale, sublicensing, redistribution or making raw guitars available to third parties without written approval.

Nothing in the public terms establishes a right to repurpose the raw guitar WAV/stem/MIDI assets as a commercial product-validation dataset/reference corpus. Even if a custom written license were theoretically negotiable, reference semantics already fail independently.

### Disposition

**Reject before purchase/media access for frozen V6 admission.**

Do not:
- buy/download a pack for V6;
- treat companion bass/chord MIDI as performed note truth;
- reconstruct guitar note events from stems;
- infer onsets from the real audio;
- contact GRAUX seeking V6 rights under the current metadata-only phase.

A different future GRAUX/rightsholder product could only be reconsidered if authoritative documentation establishes separately captured contemporaneous per-note guitar onset+pitch truth plus explicit dataset/product-validation rights.

## Selekt guitar stems — rights-oriented stems, no independent note reference

Selekt currently markets real guitar stems isolated from rights-cleared/public-domain/CC sources and documents source/license provenance. However, its surfaced product description is a stem/sampling workflow, including AI-separated real recordings and local key/BPM analysis. It does not establish independent performed guitar MIDI/onset truth.

**Disposition:** not a V6 admission candidate; rights-cleared audio alone is insufficient.

## Commercial-library pattern

The commercial search reinforces a recurring distinction:
- **sample/loop products** may have clean ownership and real guitar;
- **companion MIDI** is commonly arrangement/chord/production material rather than a separately sensed performed reference;
- licenses are usually for music-production incorporation, not raw validation-dataset use;
- commercial availability therefore does not solve the frozen independent-reference gate.

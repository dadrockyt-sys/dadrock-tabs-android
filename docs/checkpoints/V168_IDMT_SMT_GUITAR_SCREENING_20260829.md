Fraunhofer IDMT's authoritative dataset page states that the dataset is provided **for evaluation purpose under Creative Commons CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**.

This is sufficient to resolve the earlier uncertainty about the public dataset's stated use basis for non-commercial evaluation screening. It is **not** a redistribution/transformation shortcut. ## Annotation/reference evidence
### Original DAFx-2014 benchmark
Primary paper:
https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2014/dafx14_christian_kehling_automatic_tablature_trans.pdf

The paper states that its evaluation dataset:
- was **recorded and manually annotated with all note parameters discussed in the paper**;
- stores parameter annotations in XML;
- served as ground truth for the experiments;
- compares detections to manually annotated ground truth;
- includes onset, offset, pitch, string, plucking-style, and expression-style reference parameters.

This is strong evidence that the **original benchmark partitions** used human/manual ground truth rather than labels generated by the proposed transcription model.

### Current public V2 structure
Fraunhofer/Zenodo describe four subsets. Of particular interest:
- subset 3 contains **five short monophonic/polyphonic guitar recordings**;
- all five were recorded with one instrument;
- each has a parameter annotation in XML.

A 2024 SynthTab paper independently describes three IDMT subsets with string-level note annotations, respectively containing isolated notes/chords, twelve short licks, and **five short pieces**:
https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10534057

This supports the structural suitability of subset 3 as note/string annotated piece-like material, but does not establish its annotation author or validation procedure.

## Provenance gap — MATERIAL BLOCKER
A later 2024 annotation-quality paper provides the key lineage clarification:
https://www.lucaturchet.it/PUBLIC_DOWNLOADS/publications/conferences/On_the_Importance_of_Temporally_Precise_Onset_Annotations_for_Real-Time_Music_Information_Retrieval-_Findings_from_the_AG-PT-set_Dataset.pdf

It describes IDMT-SMT-Guitar as having about 5100 note events in its **two original partitions**, with the additional **five short pieces and 64 longer pieces added in following versions**. The same paper states that the IDMT authors describe a manual annotation procedure but provide only information about the annotation file format, i.e. the annotation procedure itself is not documented in enough detail.

Therefore the DAFx-2014 statement that the original evaluation dataset was manually annotated cannot safely be projected onto the later-added subset-3 five pieces without additional provenance evidence.

The inspected authoritative/public sources do **not** establish for subset 3:
- who created the five XML references;
- whether a professional musician/transcriber created or fully validated them;
- the exact annotation workflow used for onset/offset/pitch/string events;
- whether the five XML references were inherited from a score/performance plan or independently audited against the recorded audio;
- a complete professional-review chain satisfying the project's frozen `professional_scorer_ready` semantics.

The 2024 annotation-quality study did independently re-label a random sample from IDMT and reports a mean absolute onset error of about 8.5 ms for its sample. That is useful quality evidence, but it is a **sample-level timing audit**, not complete professional validation of the five subset-3 references and does not cure the provenance gap.

## Frozen-gate assessment
### Positive
- real guitar recordings;
- open authoritative package;
- explicit evaluation-use CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 statement;
- human/manual ground-truth evidence for original partitions;
- current subset 3 is piece-like and accompanied by XML;
- later literature treats subset 3 as string-level note annotated.

### Blocking
- subset 3 is a later addition, not one of the two original partitions covered unambiguously by the 2014 manual-annotation statement;
- exact subset-3 annotation preparation/validation provenance is not publicly documented strongly enough for the current frozen professional-reference gate;
- no complete professional `preparedBy`/validation identity can be frozen prospectively from inspected evidence;
- exact source/reference SHA256 pair identities have not been acquired/frozen;
- no admission validator has been run on actual assets.

## Decision
**IDMT-SMT-Guitar subset 3 is `BLOCKED / NOT ADMITTED` for V168 under the current frozen gate.**

Do not weaken `professional_scorer_ready` semantics merely because the dataset is a respected benchmark or because the original two partitions were manually annotated. New primary-source provenance specifically tying subset 3 to an adequate professional annotation/validation process would justify reopening this screening; otherwise keep it blocked.

No V168 asset admission occurred. No reference note-event content was inspected. No score call occurred.

# Lead Professional PDF Engine

Lead owns six-string professional Lead TAB engraving based on authenticated Lead events.

Lead may reuse shared six-string layout primitives, but Lead-specific technique notation and phrasing must come from the Lead analyzer evidence rather than Rhythm assumptions.

Expected inputs include authenticated measure/step placement, MIDI pitch, six-string string/fret position, duration/sustain, bends/releases, vibrato, slides, hammer-ons, pull-offs and other proven Lead techniques, plus tuning, tempo, meter, key and metadata.

This renderer remains inactive until the Lead analyzer has earned a distinct reference-free professional identity and real-audio quality evidence.
